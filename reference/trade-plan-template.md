# Trade plan template

Copy this for every trade. Fill it in before entry. Nothing after
"Entry" is written after the trade is open.

```
Pair / asset:
Direction: long / short
Timeframe of the setup:
Market structure (one sentence):
Key support:
Key resistance:

Entry:
Stop loss:
Take profit:
Position size (from calculator):
Risk if stopped out ($ and %):
Reward if target hit ($ and R):

Rationale (1-2 sentences):
Invalidation (the exact event that proves the idea wrong):

Checklist passed: yes / no (if no, this is a watchlist item, not a trade)
```

## Rules for filling it in

- **Entry** is a price, not a zone of hope. If the entry is "around
  support", wait for the confirmation and write the confirmed price.
- **Stop** goes where the idea is wrong — beyond the level, outside
  the noise. Never move the stop further away after entry to avoid
  the loss.
- **Target** is the next level in the direction of the trade, not a
  round number picked for luck.
- **Invalidation** names the event: "a 4H close below support at X"
  or "a break of the higher low at Y". If you cannot name it, you do
  not have a plan.
- **Size** comes from the calculator, from the risk percent you chose
  before looking at the chart. Never size from how confident you feel.
