# Sample trade plan

> ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE. This plan is a teaching example with invented
> levels — it is NOT a past trade and NOT a signal to copy.

```
Pair / asset: SOL/USD
Direction: long
Timeframe of the setup: 4H
Market structure (one sentence): Uptrend — higher highs and higher
  lows for two weeks.
Key support: 143 (held twice)
Key resistance: 162 (rejected once)

Entry: 145
Stop loss: 140
Take profit: 162
Position size (from calculator): 2 units
Risk if stopped out: $10 (1% of a $1,000 account)
Reward if target hit: $34 (3.4R)

Rationale: Uptrend pullback to a twice-held support, with a 4H close
  back above 145 after a rejection wick at 143.
Invalidation: a 4H close below 140 — the structure breaks and the
  idea is wrong.

Checklist passed: yes
```
