# Pre-trade checklist

Every line must pass. One fail means watchlist, not a trade.

## Structure

- [ ] I can name the structure in one sentence: uptrend, downtrend,
      range, or breakout-and-retest.
- [ ] The direction agrees with the trend, or I have written down
      why this counter-trend trade is worth it (smaller size, tighter
      stop).

## Level

- [ ] The level held more than once (two or more touches).
- [ ] The level is from the higher timeframe (daily / 4H first),
      not only the 15-minute chart.
- [ ] The level has not been tested so many times that it is worn
      out.

## Confirmation

- [ ] There is a confirmation: a close in my direction, a rejection
      wick at the level, or a break-and-retest that held.
- [ ] I am not entering in the middle of the range or mid-trend
      with no level nearby.

## Risk

- [ ] The stop is beyond the level, outside the noise — not inside
      it, not at an obvious round number everyone else uses.
- [ ] I ran the position-size calculator and the size loses exactly
      my chosen risk percent if the stop is hit.
- [ ] The target gives at least 1.5R. Below that, the trade does
      not pay for its own risk.
- [ ] The risk is money I can afford to lose. If the loss would
      hurt, the size is too big.

## Plan

- [ ] The plan is written down (pair, direction, entry, stop,
      target, size, invalidation) before I enter.
- [ ] The invalidation is a specific event, not a feeling.
